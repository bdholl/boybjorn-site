Short bio

Boy Bjorn is the solo folk project of Milwaukee songwriter Brian Holl, one half of the alt-folk duo Foreign Fields. Small songs built on acoustic guitar, banjo and close harmonies, for fans of Gregory Alan Isakov, S. Carey and Hovvdy. New single The River is out December 4, 2026.

Long bio

Boy Bjorn is the solo folk project of Milwaukee songwriter Brian Holl. Holl is one half of the alt-folk duo Foreign Fields, whose music reaches more than 150,000 listeners a month on Spotify. As Boy Bjorn, he signed to Communion Records and released his debut, Mistaken Animals, in 2019.

The songs are born out of a desire to capture moments lost: small moments built on acoustic guitar, banjo and close harmonies. Fans of Gregory Alan Isakov, S. Carey and Hovvdy will feel at home.

Boy Bjorn returns on December 4, 2026 with The River, which WMSE 91.7 FM premiered ahead of release. More new songs follow through 2027. “I’m leaning into my folky love song tendencies this time around,” Holl says.
