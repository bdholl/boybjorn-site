About

Boy Bjorn is the solo folk project of Milwaukee songwriter Brian Holl, one half of the alt-folk duo Foreign Fields. Small songs “born out of a desire to capture moments lost,” for fans of Gregory Alan Isakov, S. Carey and Hovvdy.

After signing to Communion Records and releasing the 2019 debut Mistaken Animals, Holl returns with The River on December 4. “I’m leaning into my folky love song tendencies this time around,” he says. “More honest, and more direct than ever before.”
